Privacy Policy

LYKLUND Ads Dashboard
Last updated: November 24, 2026

This policy describes how LYKLUND handles information in connection with LYKLUND Ads Dashboard (the “Application”). The Application is an internal analytics tool for LYKLUND’s own authorized Amazon advertising accounts. It is not offered as a public service for unrelated advertisers.

1. Information accessed

When an account owner authorizes the Amazon Ads integration, the Application accesses information made available through the authorized Amazon Ads API permissions. Depending on the reports requested, this may include:

  • Advertising profile and account identifiers, marketplace and currency settings.
  • Campaign, ad group, advertised product, SKU and ASIN identifiers, and advertising report dates.
  • Advertising performance metrics such as impressions, clicks, spend, attributed orders and attributed sales.
  • Authorization credentials and tokens needed to maintain the authorized connection.

The advertising integration is intended for reporting and analysis. It does not request individual shoppers’ names, delivery addresses or payment card details. The Application does not need or collect your Amazon account password; account authorization takes place with Amazon.

2. How information is used

Information is used to retrieve advertising reports, summarize advertising costs by product and date, support internal SKU performance and profitability analysis, and maintain or troubleshoot the authorized connection. Advertising data may be combined locally with LYKLUND’s own order and cost records for these purposes.

3. Storage and access

The Application is designed to run locally on a LYKLUND-controlled computer. The existing local dashboard stores its business records in an encrypted local database and uses macOS Keychain for supported connection credentials. Access to the dashboard is protected by a local login. Advertising integration credentials must be kept in secure credential storage when that integration is configured.

This public policy page is separate from the local dashboard. Advertising reports, order records and API credentials must not be uploaded to the public repository that hosts this page.

4. Sharing and service providers

LYKLUND does not sell or rent Amazon advertising information or provide it to unrelated advertisers. Access is limited to authorized LYKLUND personnel for the internal purposes described above. Information is exchanged with Amazon as necessary to authorize access and request reports.

If this policy page is hosted on GitHub Pages, GitHub provides the public website hosting and may process website access information under its own privacy practices. Hosting this policy does not give GitHub access to the locally stored dashboard records or credentials. Email providers may process messages you send to the contact address below.

5. Retention and deletion

Locally downloaded information is retained while needed for internal reporting, reconciliation and legitimate recordkeeping. Authorized account owners may request deletion of locally stored advertising reports or connection credentials using the contact address below. LYKLUND will assess and handle requests subject to applicable recordkeeping requirements.

Revoking authorization stops future authorized API access but does not automatically erase previously downloaded records. Local copies, exports and backups must also be addressed when deleting information.

6. Account owner controls

Account owners can manage or revoke the Application’s authorization through Amazon’s account or application authorization controls. For assistance disconnecting an account, correcting information or deleting local records, contact LYKLUND.

7. Policy updates

This policy may be updated as the Application or its data handling changes. The latest version will be published on this page with an updated date.
